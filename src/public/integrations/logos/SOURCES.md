# Integration logo sources

Logo artwork is stored locally. Brand names and marks belong to their respective owners.

Iconify’s Logos and Simple Icons collections distribute icon artwork under CC0; the source collections retain their own brand/trademark guidance. The remaining artwork comes from the linked vendor repositories under their respective repository terms.

`byo.svg` is original Datus artwork: a small model network with an add symbol, drawn in the Datus purple to represent bring-your-own models. It is not a third-party vendor mark.
